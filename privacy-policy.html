    Privacy Policy

    Your Privacy Matters to AVMP Elevators & Engineering

    At AVMP Elevators & Engineering, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us through our website, enquiry forms and other communication channels.

    1. Information We Collect

    When you contact us, request a service, submit an enquiry or communicate with us through our website, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, company or organisation details, location and any information you choose to provide in your message.

    We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and website usage information where required for website operation, security and improvement.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    Information submitted through our website may be used to:

    • Respond to enquiries and requests.
    • Provide information about our elevator services.
    • Arrange maintenance, inspection or technical support.
    • Communicate with you regarding requested services.
    • Improve our website, services and customer experience.
    • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.

    3. Enquiry and Contact Information

    If you submit an enquiry through our website, the information you provide may be accessed by authorised members of AVMP Elevators & Engineering for the purpose of responding to your request and providing relevant services.

    We do not use enquiry information for unrelated purposes without a relevant reason or your consent where such consent is required.

    4. Communication

    If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we may use the information provided during the communication to understand your requirement, respond to your enquiry and provide appropriate service support.

    5. Cookies and Website Technologies

    Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage and improve the overall user experience.

    You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function as intended if certain cookies are disabled.

    6. Information Security

    We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted to us against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

    7. Sharing of Information

    We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service personnel, technology providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to operate our website, respond to your enquiry or provide requested services, subject to applicable requirements.

    8. Third-Party Services

    Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms or communication services. These third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. AVMP Elevators & Engineering is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

    9. Data Retention

    We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain business records, resolve enquiries or meet applicable legal and operational requirements.

    10. Your Privacy Choices

    You may contact us if you have questions regarding the personal information you have submitted to us or if you wish to request clarification regarding its use, subject to applicable laws and reasonable verification requirements.

    11. Children's Privacy

    Our website and services are intended for general audiences and are not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for unrelated purposes.

    12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website practices or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised information.

    13. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact us using the details below:
